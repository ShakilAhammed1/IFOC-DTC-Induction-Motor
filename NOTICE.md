# Attribution and reuse status

This package contains user-supplied simulation drivers and adapted Simulink models that refer to MathWorks product libraries. MATLAB, Simulink, Simscape, and Simscape Electrical are MathWorks products. No product installers or licensed toolbox binaries are included.

Original notices inside the supplied files are retained. This packaging review does not establish the original example provenance or grant redistribution rights for third-party material. The authors should identify any source example and retain its applicable terms before release. No blanket MIT, CC, or other license has been applied to third-party-derived models.

The research authors should choose appropriate terms for their own code and data and complete author/title/citation metadata before publishing. The package is currently supplied without an additional reuse license.
